### Skill: Pre-send read

```text
Create a skill called Pre-send read.
When to use: Before any message goes to a prospect, including every follow-up.
Inputs and access: The draft, the recipient, the source for each fact about them, and the opt-out list.
Sequence:
1. Mark every sentence in the draft that states a fact about the prospect or their company.
2. Under each marked fact, paste the source line you can quote and say where it came from.
3. Cut any fact with no quotable source. Do not fill the gap with a guess.
4. Check the recipient against the opt-out list. If they are on it, stop and say so.
5. Write one line on why this matters to this person today. If you can't, the verdict is DON'T SEND.
6. Count today's sends. If the daily send limit is reached, hold the draft for tomorrow.
7. Show me the verdict, the draft and the sources. Send only after I say yes to this draft and recipient.
Validate: Every fact has a quoted source, the recipient is not opted out, and today's sends are under the limit.
Return: First line: READY, FIX or DON'T SEND. Then the draft, then one quoted source under each fact.
What requires approval: send: ask me and wait for my yes on this exact draft and recipient before anything goes out.
Also put these hard rules in the skill's approval field: Payments: forbidden. Never pay, transfer or authorize money out. Give me the details to pay myself. Sending: show me each message first; send only after my explicit yes to that exact message.
```
