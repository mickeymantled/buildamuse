```md
# Inbox triage

## When to use
I say 'triage my inbox', or unread mail has piled up since the last triage.

## Inputs
Unread mail since the last triage, and the people I told you always count as urgent.

## Steps
1. Read every unread message in scope and put each in one bucket: needs reply, needs a decision, FYI, or junk.
2. For each needs-reply message, write a draft and save it unsent.
3. For FYI and junk, list the messages you propose to archive or delete, and move none of them.
4. List every bill, invoice or payment request with sender, amount and due date, and pay none of them.
5. Number every proposed reply, archive and delete, then ask for my approval once, as a single batch.
6. Carry out only the numbers I approve, then report what was done and what is still waiting.

## Validate
Every message sits in exactly one bucket, every proposed send or delete has a number, and nothing was sent, deleted or paid before my yes.

## Returns
Verdict first: how many messages need me today. Then a numbered list of proposals grouped by bucket.

## Requires approval
send: every reply waits for my yes by number. delete: every archive or delete waits for my yes by number. pay is forbidden: bills are listed, never paid.
```

### project/conflict-scan.md (Skill: Conflict scan, file)

```md
# Conflict scan

## When to use
An invite, booking request or reschedule arrives, or I ask 'does this fit' before agreeing to a time.

## Inputs
The proposed time, duration and attendees, my calendar for that day and the day either side, and my working hours from memory.

## Steps
1. Check the proposed slot against every event on my calendar, tentative ones and travel time included.
2. Check the slot against my working hours and any day I marked as blocked.
3. If the slot is clear, say so. If it clashes, name each clashing event with its time and attendees.
4. Propose up to three alternative slots that clear every clash, nearest first.
5. Draft the reply (accept, decline or counter) and save it unsent.
6. Ask me for approval by number, then send, accept, decline, move, reschedule or cancel only the numbers I approve.

## Validate
Every clash names an event, a time and attendees, every alternative is clear of my calendar, and no event was created, moved, rescheduled, accepted, declined or cancelled before my yes.

## Returns
Verdict first: CLEAR or CLASH. Then the clashes, up to three alternative slots, and the unsent draft reply.

## Requires approval
send: accepting, declining, countering, creating, moving or rescheduling an event sends a message or update notice and waits for my yes. delete: cancelling or removing an event waits for my yes.
```
